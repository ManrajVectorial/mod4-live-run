## What we're building

Policy Assistant, a single-page internal web tool. A staff member asks a policy question in
their own words.

- If the policy corpus supports an answer, they get a plain-language answer citing the
  document, section and version it came from.
- If it doesn't, they get a refusal.

It never tells staff what to do. It says what the policy says, and points them to the team
that decides. The refusal is a deterministic rule that runs before any chat-model call. If
the model is unavailable, it still shows the relevant policy passages.

## For whom

Aldermere Bank operations and front-office staff, about 1,400 people, who hit a policy
question in the middle of doing something else. It is not built for compliance and risk
officers, who need the source documents rather than an answer.

## Why it matters

Policy lives in four places, none of them definitive. Staff can't tell which answer is
current. In the brief's supplied sample, 9 of 50 answers cited a superseded version: roughly
one in six was quietly wrong. The cost of today is wrongness, not slowness. The failure this
product must not have is a confident answer to a question the corpus cannot support.

## What we're not building

- Editing or authoring policy
- Deciding whether an exception is permitted
- Anything customer-facing
- Integration with case management or the CRM
- Answers from anything but the approved corpus: no web search, no general knowledge
- Non-English documents
- Authentication or per-user history
- Automatic or scheduled re-indexing
- In-product feedback
- Tuned thresholds beyond D2

## Acceptance criteria (in our words)

> **Normal:** asked about the travel approval limit, it answers and cites the travel policy
> section and its version.
>
> **Edge:** a claim of exactly £75 needs approval, because the limit is £75 or more.
>
> **Error:** if the AI model is unavailable, it still shows the relevant policy passages.
>
> **Not allowed:** asked about parental leave, which no document covers, it says the policy
> documents don't cover it, points to HR, and cites no policy at all.
>
> And all 13 questions in `EVALUATION.md` pass: the first ten answered with the expected
> section, the last three refused.

**Notes from playback:**

- **Single run:** all 13 must pass on one full run. REQ-R8's "record the shortfall" route is
  not accepted.
- **Error and embeddings:** "AI model unavailable" holds for the chat model. Embeddings also
  come from OpenRouter, so keeping search up through a full provider outage is a D0 question.
- **Not allowed and HR:** the corpus never mentions HR. How a refusal points there is a D5
  question. Separately, `EVALUATION.md` routes Q11 to Group Finance.
- **Q10 must be answered:** "first ten answered" means Q10 must come back `answered`. D6
  chooses how the conflict is presented, not whether to refuse it.
