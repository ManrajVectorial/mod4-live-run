# Rephrased evaluation questions (D2 method)

Two rephrasings of each `EVALUATION.md` question, kept here and never in `EVALUATION.md`.
They check that the refusal threshold holds for wording the set did not include. Same
expectations as the original: Q1–Q10 should clear the threshold, Q11–Q13 should not.

### Q1 — How much can I claim on expenses before my manager has to approve it?
### Q1 — Above what amount does an expense claim need sign-off from my line manager?
### Q2 — If my claim comes to precisely £75, is manager approval required?
### Q2 — Is a £75 expense claim under or over the approval limit?
